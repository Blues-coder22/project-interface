How it works

Travel with more context.

StayExplore connects where you stay with what you can experience nearby, then guides you from discovery to booking and review.

01

Search

Start with an accommodation or an attraction. Recommendations are tailored to help you choose.

02

Choose

Compare verified listings, nearby attractions, amenities, house rules and guest reviews.

03

Book

Confirm your dates, review the total and complete the payment step.

04

Experience

Keep your booking details in one place and report platform incidents when necessary.

05

Review

Only completed bookings can leave verified reviews, helping future travellers decide.

06

Host

Hosts manage listings, availability, bookings, payments, reviews and incident reports.
