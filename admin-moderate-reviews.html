Risk score (0–100). A rule-based guide that helps you decide what to check first. It looks at signals such as a host report, links or phone numbers in the text, identical text, several reviews in minutes, very short or all-capitals text, and a rating far from the listing's average. Hover a badge to see the reasons. It is a signal, not proof, and nothing is removed automatically.
Reviews come only from completed bookings. Guest and host reviews stay sealed until both are in, or the 14-day window closes.
