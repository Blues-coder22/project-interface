Support

Help centre.

Answers to common questions about booking, hosting and your account.

How do I book a stay?

Find a stay, choose your dates, select Reserve and sign in or create a tourist account. Then confirm your details and complete payment.

Can I browse without an account?

Yes. Stays and attractions are open to everyone. You only need an account to book, save favourites, review or report.

How do I become a host?

Create a host account, add your listing and submit it. An administrator reviews it before it is shown to tourists.

Who can leave a review?

Only guests with a completed booking can leave a verified review.

How do I report a problem?

Sign in and use Report an incident. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.
