About StayExplore Kenya

Discover more. Stay better.

StayExplore Kenya is a travel discovery and accommodation platform designed to help travellers find suitable places to stay and discover attractions nearby.

Kenya first Built around local stays, destinations and experiences.
Our purpose

Make travel decisions easier.

Travellers often have to search for accommodation and attractions separately. StayExplore brings those decisions together so a traveller can start with a stay or an attraction and continue naturally through the journey.

Our vision

Connect people with places.

We aim to make discovering Kenya more convenient while giving local hosts an organised way to present properties, manage bookings and connect with travellers.

What we offer

One connected travel experience

Smart discovery

Accommodation and attraction results are arranged to help you decide with confidence.

Trusted information

Verified-booking reviews and listing moderation are designed to make decisions clearer and safer.

Local hosting

Hosts can present their properties, manage availability and bookings, and keep track of their platform activity.

For travellers

Plan with context.

Compare stays, explore nearby attractions, check amenities and reviews, make a booking, and keep your trip information in one place.
